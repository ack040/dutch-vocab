How to use it
  1. Pick your colour. Only traps you can spring are shown; nothing warns you about your own mistakes.
  2. Enter every move as it is played: tap a piece and then its square, type it, or tap a suggested move.
  3. Blue arrow: your book move. Red arrow: the losing move the opponent might play. Green arrow: the move that punishes it.
  4. Traps are matched by position, so a different move order that reaches the same position still counts.

Traps

Every line is replayed move by move for legality, and every trap was checked with Stockfish at depth 20. Your colour, the game you are entering and the traps you have learned are kept in this browser only.
